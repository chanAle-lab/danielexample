# 🏛️ ABRSM Music Theory 101: Grades 1 to 5

> An interactive, audio-enabled music theory learning platform built completely around the **Official ABRSM Music Theory Syllabus (2020+): Grades 1 to 5**.

🌐 **Live Website on GitHub Pages**: [https://chanale-lab.github.io/MusicTheory101/](https://chanale-lab.github.io/MusicTheory101/)

## 📚 Complete Syllabus Alignment (Grades 1 to 5)

This web application covers every single point mandated by the **Associated Board of the Royal Schools of Music (ABRSM)**:

### 🎓 Grade 1
- **Note Values & Rests**: Semibreve (whole note), Minim (half), Crotchet (quarter), Quaver (8th), Semiquaver (16th), tied notes, and single-dotted notes.
- **Simple Time Signatures**: $\frac{2}{4}$, $\frac{3}{4}$, $\frac{4}{4}$, bar-lines and correct note/rest grouping.
- **The Stave & Clefs**: Treble ($G$) and Bass ($F$) clefs, Middle C reference, sharps ($\sharp$), flats ($\flat$), and naturals ($\natural$).
- **Major Scales & Keys**: C, G, D, and F Major scales ($T-T-S-T-T-T-S$), tonic triads (root position), degrees (1–7), and intervals above the tonic (by number only).
- **Terms & Signs**: Tempo, dynamics, performance directions, and articulation.

### 🎓 Grade 2
- **Simple Time Signatures**: $\frac{2}{2}$, $\frac{3}{2}$, $\frac{4}{2}$ (minim beat) and $\frac{3}{8}$ (quaver beat).
- **Triplets**: Triplet notes and rest grouping.
- **Ledger Lines**: Two ledger lines above and below each stave.
- **Relative Keys & Minor Scales**: A, E, D Harmonic Minor scales, A, $B\flat$, $E\flat$ Major keys.

### 🎓 Grade 3
- **Compound Time Signatures**: $\frac{6}{8}$, $\frac{9}{8}$, $\frac{12}{8}$ and compound grouping rules.
- **Demisemiquaver**: 32nd notes and equivalent rests.
- **Transposition at the Octave**: Between Treble and Bass clefs.
- **Keys up to 4 Sharps/Flats**: Both **Harmonic and Melodic** minor forms, tonic triads, and interval classification (Number and Quality: Major, Minor, Perfect).

### 🎓 Grade 4
- **Time & Rhythms**: All simple and compound duple, triple, and quadruple times; Breve (double whole note), double-dotted notes, and duplets.
- **Alto Clef ($C$ clef centered on line 3)**: Reading Viola music, octave transposition across Treble, Bass, and Alto clefs.
- **Double Sharps ($\boldsymbol{\sharp\sharp}$ / $\boldsymbol{\mathbb{X}}$) & Double Flats ($\boldsymbol{\flat\flat}$)**: Cancellation and enharmonic equivalents.
- **Keys up to 5 Sharps/Flats**: Diatonic scale technical degree names: **Tonic, Supertonic, Mediant, Subdominant, Dominant, Submediant, Leading Note**.
- **Chromatic Scales & Triads**: Triads and chords in root position on Tonic ($I$), Subdominant ($IV$), and Dominant ($V$).
- **Ornaments**: Recognition, audio demonstration, and notation of **Trill**, **Turn**, **Upper Mordent**, **Lower Mordent**, **Acciaccatura**, and **Appoggiatura**.
- **Standard Orchestral Instruments**: String, Woodwind, Brass, and Percussion families.

### 🎓 Grade 5 (Capstone Level)
- **Irregular Time Signatures**: $\frac{5}{4}$, $\frac{7}{4}$, $\frac{5}{8}$, $\frac{7}{8}$ and irregular divisions (quintuplets, sextuplets, septuplets).
- **Tenor Clef ($C$ clef centered on line 4)**: Cello, Bassoon, and Trombone higher registers, identification across all 4 clefs.
- **Transposing Instruments**: Transposing to and from concert pitch for instruments in $B\flat$ (Clarinet, Trumpet), $A$ (Clarinet in A), and $F$ (French Horn, Cor Anglais).
- **All Keys up to 6 Sharps/Flats**: Complete scale system and compound intervals (9ths, 10ths, etc.).
- **Chord Inversions**: Root position ($a$ / 5-3), First inversion ($b$ / 6-3), and Second inversion ($c$ / 6-4) forms of $I$, $ii$, $IV$, and $V$.
- **Cadences**: **Perfect Cadence** ($V \to I$), **Plagal Cadence** ($IV \to I$), and **Imperfect Cadence** ($I \to V$, $ii \to V$, $IV \to V$) in C, G, D, and F Major with one-click audio playback.
- **Score Reading & Vocal Ranges**: Soprano, Alto, Tenor, Bass (SATB), instrument clefs, and sound production.

## 🛠️ Interactive Features
- **🎼 Multi-Clef Stave Engine (SVG)**: Dynamically renders Treble, Bass, Alto, and Tenor clefs, ledger lines, and notes.
- **🃏 Interactive Note Flashcards (3D Flipping)**: Notes on stave on the front with live audio playback, note name, stave position, and virtual piano guidance on the back.
- **🎹 Integrated Virtual Piano**: Bidirectionally syncs with the stave and audio engine.
- **🏛️ Cadences & Inversions Audio Player**: Audition harmonic progressions in multiple keys.
- **📖 Searchable Terms & Signs Glossary**: Comprehensive Italian, French, and German musical terminology dictionary with Grade 1–5 filters.
- **📝 ABRSM Practice Exam Simulator**: Real exam questions with explanations and streak tracking.
